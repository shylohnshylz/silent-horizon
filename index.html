About us

A quiet studio
at the edge of the visible.

Somewhere between the event horizon and the first impression, a decision is made about your company. Silent Horizon exists to make that decision inevitable.

We are a compact team of designers and engineers who treat the web as a medium, not a template. Our work begins with strategy and typography, moves through interaction design, and ends in hand-written, performance-audited code that loads in the blink of an eye — on a phone, in a tunnel, on a five-year-old laptop.

No page builders. No recycled layouts. No layers of plugins pretending to be craft. Just a small number of senior people working directly with you from the first sketch to the final deploy.
